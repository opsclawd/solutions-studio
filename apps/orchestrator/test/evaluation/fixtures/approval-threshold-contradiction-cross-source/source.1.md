# 1. Governance & Capital Expenditure

This enterprise policy defines mandatory financial controls and executive reservation of authority for capital asset acquisitions across all corporate entities and subsidiaries.

# 2. Threshold Classifications

All capital expenditures must be categorized by aggregate spend commitment prior to contract execution.

# 3. Executive Approval Limits

Section 3.2: Capital expenditures exceeding $10,000 require formal approval from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) prior to vendor contract execution.
