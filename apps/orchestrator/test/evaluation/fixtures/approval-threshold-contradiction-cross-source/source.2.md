# 1. Field Equipment Acquisition Workflow

Field maintenance operations require rapid turnaround for replacement machinery and tooling to minimize production line downtime.

Section 1.5: Field capital asset purchases up to $50,000 may be approved solely by the Operations Director without CFO review, enabling immediate operational dispatch.

# 2. Emergency Tooling

Emergency tooling replacements follow identical expedited approval limits under the supervision of local site managers.
