# 1. Travel Authorization & Scope

Employees traveling on official company business are entitled to reimbursement for reasonable meal and lodging expenses.

# 2. Meal Reimbursement Tiers

Section 4.1: For domestic travel within North America, meal expenses are reimbursed up to a maximum per diem of $75 USD.

Section 4.2: For international travel outside North America, meal expenses are reimbursed up to a maximum per diem of $150 USD.
