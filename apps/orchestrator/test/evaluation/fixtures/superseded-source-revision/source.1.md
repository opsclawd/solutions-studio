# 1. Commercial Scope

This procedure outlines pricing discretion boundaries for account executives and commercial sales managers closing enterprise subscription contracts.

# 2. Discretionary Approval Limits

Section 2.1: Commercial sales managers may approve discretionary customer pricing discounts of up to 25% off list price without executive escalation.
