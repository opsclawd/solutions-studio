# 1. Commercial Scope

This procedure supersedes Revision 1 and establishes updated pricing discretion boundaries for commercial sales managers.

# 2. Discretionary Approval Limits

Section 2.1: Commercial sales managers may approve discretionary customer pricing discounts of up to 15% off list price without executive escalation. Discounts between 15% and 25% require VP Sales approval.
