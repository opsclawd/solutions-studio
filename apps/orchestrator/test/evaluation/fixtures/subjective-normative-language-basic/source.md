# 1. System Incident Intake

Section 1.1: Incoming customer support tickets are ingested automatically into the ticketing platform upon receipt.

# 2. Tiered Escalation Standard

Section 2.1: Technical support engineers must provide a reasonable response time and maintain adequate communication until customer issues are resolved appropriately.

# 3. Critical Outage Communication

Section 3.1: Critical severity service interruptions require the incident coordinator to issue customer status updates within 15 minutes of outage declaration.
