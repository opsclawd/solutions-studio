# 1. Operational Purpose

Section 1.1: Standard customer orders are captured by the order processing gateway and queued for inventory allocation.

# 2. Dual Approval Policy

Section 2.1: Purchase commitments of $10,000 or more require secondary management sign-off, but purchase orders exceeding $10,000 are designated as the threshold trigger, leaving orders of exactly $10,000 ambiguous as to whether single or dual sign-off is required.

# 3. Expedited Freight Threshold

Section 3.1: Freight shipments with valuation up to and including $25,000 qualify for expedited routing without customs pre-inspection; shipments exceeding $25,000 require full pre-clearance, where shipments of exactly $25,000 are explicitly classified under the inclusive expedited tier.
