# 1. Purpose & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure establishes approval authority requirements for all commercial purchase orders and vendor commitments across operating business units.

# 2. Procurement Thresholds

Section 2.1: Purchase orders up to and including $50,000 may be authorized solely by designated team managers without Department Vice President approval; Department Vice President sign-off is required only for commitments exceeding $50,000 before submission to the accounting department.

# 3. Standard Requisitions

Section 3.1: Standard requisitions below the executive threshold may be approved by designated team managers with budget allocation authority.

# 4. Department Sign-Off Rules

Section 4.3: Any purchase order with a total value greater than $25,000 requires written sign-off from the Department Vice President before commitment.
