# 1. Removable Media Standards

This policy governs all portable data storage media interacting with corporate devices, grid telemetry, and operational networks.

Section 1.1: Under no circumstances may unencrypted removable storage devices be connected to company systems. All data transfer in transit must use encrypted, corporate-managed channels and hardware-encrypted USB storage.
